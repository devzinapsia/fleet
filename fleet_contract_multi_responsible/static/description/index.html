Responsables múltiples y aviso de vencimiento de contratos de flota

Share vehicle contracts between several responsibles and email all of them before they expire

Adds an Additional responsibles field to vehicle contracts, next to the existing responsible. A daily scheduled action then emails every responsible of each running contract a few days before it expires (5 by default, configurable in the Fleet settings): one email per person, in their own language, from the contract's company, with the contract details and a button to open it. Each expiration date is notified only once, and a new date is notified again automatically.

  • Searchable field, available as an optional column in the contracts list.
  • Inactive users and users without email are skipped and logged.
  • An internal note on the contract lists who was notified.
  • Works alongside Fleet's own "Contract to Renew" activity, without changing it.
